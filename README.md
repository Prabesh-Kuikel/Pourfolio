# Pourfolio — GitHub Pages package

This ZIP is a ready-to-publish static site. Upload its contents—`index.html`, `assets/`, and `atlas/`—to the root of a GitHub repository. Keep `atlas/` beside the main page.

## Publish
1. Upload and commit every archive entry to the repository root.
2. In GitHub, open **Settings → Pages**.
3. Select **Deploy from a branch**, choose the branch and `/(root)`, then save.
4. Open the published HTTPS URL after GitHub Pages finishes deploying.

## Navigation and search
Workspace sections and individual lessons have stable hash URLs, so they can be bookmarked or shared; browser back/forward returns to the prior section and scroll position. Use the **Search** button or Ctrl/⌘+K from anywhere. Quick Find searches lesson drills and practicums, glossary exercises, business assignments, casebook debriefs, eight Guest Service scenarios, cuisine field notes and cocktail recipes; results show matching context. Select a service scenario to open its exact role-play exercise, or choose a business topic, glossary term or cuisine note to jump to it. The local search index, large recipe catalog and cuisine notes load only when needed.

## Learning content
Includes 120 open lessons with supervised field practicums and saved four-dimension performance self-reviews; 94 glossary entries, each with a distinct practical task; 25 business topics, each with a unique evidence artifact and three observable checks; 100 quiz questions linked to ten matching domain-specific field drills; 15 open-response shift scenarios with a five-dimension response rubric; eight Guest Service role-plays with situation-specific observable self-checks, example language and explicit safety/authority boundaries; a pairing experiment worksheet; a 12-route beverage atlas with 200 cocktails and family-specific execution labs; and 13 cited cuisine/service field notes on Nepali, Chinese, Indian, French, Italian, Spanish, Romanian, Middle Eastern, Mexican, Japanese, Thai and Mediterranean foodways, plus the context-dependent “continental” label. Quiz results offer direct next-study links for each domain. Cuisine notes connect origins and culinary foundations with guest explanation, bartending and a respectful field exercise; each note explains the scope of its cited sources. Practice results record learning—not certification or workplace authorization.

## Sources, privacy and limitations
The cuisine notes are source-linked editorial summaries, **not independently reviewed by culinary historians or relevant community experts**. Cultural identities and food origins can be layered, shared or contested; use the scope notes and consult relevant community knowledge for claims that matter. Progress, notes, planning values, rubric marks and inventory remain in the current browser's `localStorage`; they are **not encrypted, uploaded or synced**. Guest role-play drafts stay only on the page and are not saved. Optional practice logs contain a scenario title and self-check count—not the draft response. Protect backups because they can contain private planning notes. Do not store passwords, payment details, credentials or sensitive guest/staff information.

This is a static, single-user study tool—not audited inventory/accounting software, a POS integration, legal/medical/accounting advice, or a food-safety system. Exercises do not replace venue SOPs, current local law, required certifications or qualified professional guidance. Follow official local requirements and authorized workplace procedures. The Atlas is same-origin and is not a security sandbox; GitHub Pages does not provide this project with custom security headers.
